# SYSTEM FAILURE

**SYSTEM FAILURE** is a cooperative multiplayer sci-fi game set aboard the **LUNARIS-01**.

Two players awaken aboard a spacecraft controlled by AURA, the ship's artificial intelligence. After a catastrophic failure separates the crew, both players must communicate, coordinate, and operate different ship systems to survive.

> Easy to understand. Difficult to coordinate. Hilarious to fail.

## 🎮 Game Concept

SYSTEM FAILURE is designed around asymmetric cooperation.

Players share the same emergency, but they do not always have access to the same information or controls. Solving failures aboard the LUNARIS-01 requires communication between both crew members.

Planned ship systems include:

- Hull integrity
- Electrical power
- Oxygen and ventilation
- Reactor systems
- Cameras and security
- Emergency systems

And potentially a few unexpected biological problems aboard the ship...

## 🤖 AURA

AURA is the artificial intelligence aboard the LUNARIS-01.

She assists the crew, provides information about ship systems, reacts to emergencies, and occasionally offers commentary on the crew's questionable survival decisions.

`AURA: All systems operational. Probably. ^_^`
